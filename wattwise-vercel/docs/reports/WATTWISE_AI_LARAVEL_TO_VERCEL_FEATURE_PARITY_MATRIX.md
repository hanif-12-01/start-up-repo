## Summary

- `PORT`: deterministic analysis, business profile/edit lifecycle, meter derivation/OCR, appliance CRUD, CSV export, plans/trial/sandbox billing, and functional themes.
- `ADAPT`: landing, dashboard, auth, report print-to-PDF, existing operational flows, settings, and framework-appropriate UX.
- `ALREADY_PARITY`: accepted onboarding and health capability.
- `DEFER_INTERNAL_ONLY`: Laravel ML demo and provider-specific plumbing.
- Explicit customer-facing gap: passkeys remain unimplemented because no official supported Better Auth integration was authorized; no custom WebAuthn cryptography was introduced.
